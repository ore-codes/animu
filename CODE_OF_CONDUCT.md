# Code of Conduct

Animu is a small project for people who like anime and want to keep enjoying it. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to be kind and to assume good faith.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep spoilers out of issue titles. Put plot details for a bad match behind a spoiler tag or in the body, and say which show and episode it concerns.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, doxxing or sharing private information.
- Sexual content or attention that is unwelcome.
- Spamming, advertising or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's Security tab or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.
